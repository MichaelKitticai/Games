Getting the best result

  1. Shoot in good light. WhatsApp's compressor struggles most with dark, grainy footage.
  2. Process here, then use Share and pick WhatsApp, or Save and post from your gallery.
  3. In WhatsApp, tap the HD button at the top of the editor before posting (if you chose HD mode).
  4. On Android, turn on Settings → Storage and data → Media upload quality → HD quality.
  5. Avoid adding stickers or text inside WhatsApp's editor; that forces an extra re-encode.
